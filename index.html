Why Do We Explore Space?

Space is not just a distant dark void, it is the future of humanity and a natural extension of our curiosity.,
For thousands of years we have looked at the stars and wondered, and today that dream has become reality.
Every rocket we launch carries our dreams with it,and every star we study teaches us something new about the
origin of the universe. Space exploration has given us satellites, the internet, and technology that changed our daily lives,
and it still holds secrets about the possibility of other life
and the future of humans beyond Earth.
